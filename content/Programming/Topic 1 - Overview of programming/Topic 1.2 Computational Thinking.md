### Flowchart

Why and what is a flowchart

- Structured steps help simplify processes.
- Flowcharts visually represent logic clearly
- A flowchart is a visual representation of the steps involved in problem solving.

Benefits of using a flowchart

1. Clarity
   - Flowcharts offer a clear and visual representation of processes, effectively eliminating ambiguity and confusion
1. Efficiency
   - By mapping out processes, you can identify bottlenecks and areas of improvement. This leads to streamlined workflows.
1. Communication
   - Flowcharts serve as a universal language for teams, facilitating better understanding between departments.

Tips for an effective flowchart:

1. Keep it simple
   - Be clear and use simple words
   - Don't overfill the flowchart with details
2. Use standard symbols
   - Use common flowchart symbols so that everyone can understand
3. Be consistent
   - Keep shapes and sizes the same throughout
4. Review and refine
   - Update the flowcharts regularly to show changes and improvements
