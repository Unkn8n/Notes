# Composition of Functions

The composition of a function $f$ with another function g is denoted by $f o g$ (read “$f$ composed with $g$”) and is denoted by

>$(fog)(x) = f (g(x))$

A function maps every element in the domain to exactly one corresponding element in the range.

![[DomainRange.png]]

Suppose a there is a sale of 20% for a rack of clothes in a department store.
Let $x$ correspond to the original price of each item on the rack.

The function $g(x) = 0.80x$ represents the current sale price of each time.

You have been invited to a special sale that adds on another 10% off and an additional $5 off for every item at checkout.

The function $f(g(x)) = 0.9g(x) - 5$ determines the checkout price.

![[SalesexampleFunction.png]]

Q: What is the final price at the checkout when the original price of a shirt is $150?

A: Sale price:
20% off original price = $g(x) = 0.80x$
0.80(150) = $120

Additional:
10% off and $5 off = $f(x) = 0.90g(x) - 5$
0.90(120) - 5 = $103
